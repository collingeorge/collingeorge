# Collin B. George

Pre-medical student working full time in patient care at the University of Washington. I came to medicine after more than fifteen years in security and national-security work, first through independent research and then as an engineer at Pacific Northwest National Laboratory and the U.S. Department of State. I bring that work's habits with me: find how systems fail, design for human error and keep an honest record.

**Portfolio:** [collinbgeorge.com](https://collinbgeorge.com) · **MCAT study app:** [mcat.collinbgeorge.com](https://mcat.collinbgeorge.com)

## Medicine
- **[MCAT study app](https://github.com/collingeorge/MCAT):** I built it for myself and for people like me: career-changers and working adults studying around full-time jobs. It teaches the method behind each question type step by step. It's free and needs no account.
- **[Anesthesia simulator](https://collinbgeorge.com/simulator/):** a browser-based simulator I built to learn anesthesia pharmacology, with fictional patients and a published source for every dose range. A learning project, not a medical device. ([source](https://github.com/collingeorge/Smart-NOA-Controller))
- **[Clinical learning portfolio](https://github.com/collingeorge/CM-2026):** fictional critical-care cases and evidence reviews in pain management, with every source checked and the limits stated.
- **Literature reviews:** [TKA perioperative analgesia](https://github.com/collingeorge/tka-perioperative-review) · [Breast reconstruction analgesia](https://github.com/collingeorge/Breast-Reconstruction-Analgesia-Review). These are narrative reviews written as a student.
